Before you begin

Is this a good time to start?

It's the question a panchang exists to answer. Panchang answers it the moment you open it: which choghadiya is running, and whether it's Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda or Gulika, for the city you live in.

Today, sunrise to sunsetDelhi

    Every morning

    The whole day, at a glance

    Tithi, nakshatra and month at the top. Sunrise, sunset and Brahma Muhurat below. One look before you leave the house is enough.

    Rahu Kaal, Yamaganda and Gulika are worked out from your city's own sunrise, never from a fixed table.

    Vrat and parana

    Keep every vrat, and break it on time

    A reminder the evening before each Ekadashi, and the exact parana window the next morning, so the fast is kept the way it should be.

    Purnima, Amavasya, Pradosh, Sankashti and the year's festivals too, with what to do and what to avoid on each.

    Your language

    In your language, even without internet

    Everything is calculated on the phone itself, so it works in the temple, on the train, anywhere. Large text for easy reading, and a night theme for the evening aarti.

    A calendar for the month, and a muhurat finder

    Every tithi of the month

    Each day shows its tithi. Vrats, Purnima and Amavasya are marked, and any date opens its full panchang.

    Shubh muhurat, by occasion

    Good dates for a wedding, griha pravesh, a new vehicle or property, naming and mundan, with the reason for each.
